Frequently Asked Questions

How do I buy an item?

Register as a Buyer, open a product and tap "WhatsApp Seller". Agree on a price and meet on campus.

How do I sell something?

Register as a Seller, fill in the listing form with a title, price, description and photo, then post it.

Is payment handled by this site?

No. Payment is made directly between students (cash or UPI) at the campus meetup. Inspect items before paying.

Safety tips

Meet only inside campus in public places like the canteen or library, and never pay in advance.

I forgot my password

Send us a message using the form with your registered email and we will help you.
